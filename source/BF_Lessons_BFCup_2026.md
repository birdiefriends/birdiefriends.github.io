# BF Cup 2026 — lessons, observations and what to reuse for BFE (written 2026-11-11, close of Dev-98)

The BF Cup (Sat 11/7 Fourball + Greensomes, Sun 11/8 singles, 16 players, Team Jake 8.5 v Team Nate 7.5, Blue Shamrock) was the first real multi-round, multi-format BFE event. It ran live on the real Worker, engine and panel. This file records what that taught us. The step-by-step history is in `BF_Session_Log.md` (Dev-98 entries); the next start list is `BF_NextSession_Dev99.md`.

## What worked (keep doing it)
- **Pure engine, thin pages.** Every number (strokes, hole winners, points, CTP, skins, money, no-show walkover) comes from `bf_engine.js` functions fed with one config object. The panel, leaderboard, Money tab and results page all render from that. When Brian asked "check the payouts", the arithmetic was re-derivable from the config alone, and it tied to the dollar.
- **Real-stack tests.** Tests run the real pages in jsdom against the real Worker on an in-memory SQLite D1 with the real engine. They caught two live bugs this session: the ‹ › hole arrows threw a ReferenceError in production (closure variable `allowed` was out of scope in the click handler), and a "first post" flag was out of scope in the post handler. Both only fail when the code runs, never in a syntax check.
- **Build contingencies dark.** The no-show backup (walkover engine, Saturday mulligans, Sunday gross target, host-only No-show plan tab) and the Brief checklist were built, tested and hidden behind host mode + PIN. Brian's rule: "keep ready but hidden unless needed, someone will get the idea they can stay home." Nobody no-showed, and nothing leaked to players.
- **Design review before code on anything players touch.** The closest-to-pin card went from "card in the score flow" to "prompt after Post" to "dialog over the page" in three short rounds with Brian. Each step came from one sentence of his ("more words is not helpful, people don't read fine print on the course"). The final flow is minimal words, one tap.
- **Stage, then "merge it".** Staging on `claude-staging`, Brian's explicit "merge it" (or "build and merge" when he pre-authorizes), then confirm the Pages deploy with `gh run list`. The permission classifier blocks a merge that lacks that word, and that is the right behaviour for something every phone loads.
- **Worker changes delivered as the file.** Brian deploys the Worker himself (`SendUserFile` with the whole file). Never ask him to paste a patch.
- **Mock-up before building a flow he cannot see live.** He could not exercise the mulligan/range panels on a real 3-some, so a mock-up (`/tmp/BF_Cup_noshow_mockup.html`, sent) gave him the picture. He said "quick glance, looks good".

## What went wrong (and the rule that prevents it)
1. **I stated a rule I had not been told.** I wrote "hand out the Sunday draw before play" into the Brief; Brian corrected: the singles are drawn after Saturday. Rule: in anything host-facing, only state what Brian said or the config says. Mark guesses as guesses, or leave them out.
2. **Vocabulary mismatch: "match".** The app showed 4 matches on Saturday (each pairing playing a Fourball nine and a Greensomes nine). Brian counts 8, each worth a point, Ryder Cup style. I answered the wrong question first ("matches 5-8 appear after 1-4?"). Rule: when a count or name does not match his, restate the structure in his words before answering. Fixed in engine 1.49.0 (`cupMatchNumbers` counts every part of a session: Fourball 1-4, Greensomes 5-8, singles from 9).
3. **I coupled the rail to an unrelated switch.** Photo/Video/Upload on the At-the-course rail were gated on `memoriesCaptureOpen`, which exists for the Trip Memories banner. Flipping it on to see the rail also raised a redundant banner. Rule: do not hang a feature's visibility on a config that was made for something else. The rail is now always on; the banner keeps its switch.
4. **Reading the live record from the sandbox.** The sandbox cannot reach the Worker (allowlist), so I read `/bfe/cup` through WebFetch, which summarizes a ~37,000-character page. Sunday's scores sit late in it and four reads said "no Day 2 scores" while the Money tab plainly had them. Fix that worked: read with `offset`, ask for a markdown table (not verbatim JSON; the tool caps quotes at 125 characters), and cross-check against a figure the app already shows (here 5-3 and the 7 skins). Better fix for next time: a host-only export/audit view (see "Reuse" below), so nobody reads raw JSON through a summarizer.
5. **A stale phone looks like a bug.** Brian's iPad showed "Match 7" after the renumbering and a rail without Note after the rail change: old page, old script. Rule: say "hard-refresh" with every merge that changes a page, and put the build stamp (engine and panel version) somewhere visible on the Cup page so a stale phone is obvious. Not built yet.
6. **Live testing leaves live data.** Brian posted hole 10 on the real Cup to try the flow, then had to Clear hole. The Cup has a practice copy for this; use it for any test that posts scores.
7. **A long test suite blocks the turn.** `run_all.mjs` takes over 2 minutes. Run per-file with `timeout`, or in the background and read the output file, and list only the failures.
8. **Pages deploy stalled once (11/7).** Check `gh run list`; re-run a failed job, or push a trivial staged commit. Report the state honestly if it is still running.

## Observations about the event itself
- **Players tolerate one tap, not two.** Post hole then a closest-to-pin tap worked. Anything that needs reading did not get read.
- **Saturday is fully determined by the engine:** I recomputed all 8 nines from the gross scores (course handicap per tee tier, 80% Fourball off the low man, Greensomes 50% of the team gap) and got the posted 3.5-4.5. Sunday: recomputed 8 singles = 5-3, skins = the 7 on the Money tab.
- **One data slip survived:** Walli's gross 1 on the par-4 2nd (Saturday Fourball, match 2). It could not change the result (it only favoured the side that lost the nine), but nothing in the panel flagged a 1 on a par 4. A "this score looks off" warning for a gross at or below par minus 2 on a par 4/5 would catch it.
- **"Out" (0) counts:** Saturday had 12 Outs across 7 players. They count as net 99 and score correctly; they are also the best signal that a player picked up.
- **Money:** Saturday $5 ante, pot $80, CTP $5 per player = $80 over five par 3s = $16 a hole, nothing left. Sunday $15 ante, pot $240, CTP $80 ($16 a hole, hole 3 unclaimed so $16 rolled into skins), skins pot $176 over 7 skins = $25 each, $1 unallocated. Weekend: $320 collected, $319 paid, $1 left. The engine floors every split; the leftover is reported but the Money tab does not show it as a line.
- **The Money tab was judged "poor" by Brian:** it lists only winners (the 8 players who owe $20 do not appear), has no collect / paid out / left-over line, and splits each round across three tables. Proposed (not built): one weekend list of all 16 players (paid in, won, net) plus the three-number reconciliation, round detail underneath.
- **Handicap source:** the Cup keeps its own copy of the roster handicaps (refreshed only before the draft). Brian updated BFE-A and nothing changed in the Cup, which is what produced the host-only "Refresh handicaps from the event roster" button. Cup and GHIN indexes can differ; the Handicaps tab is the reference in a dispute.
- **Singles strokes:** 80% of the course-handicap gap to the higher player (Lou 14 v Nate 10: 3 strokes, on the holes ranked 1, 2, 3 = holes 2, 12, 6). Players ask; the one-paragraph explanation in the session of 11/7 is the reusable script.

## Thoughts on reusing this for BFE (the rearchitecture)
1. **The Cup is the template for "event with rounds".** Its shape: event > rounds > segments (format + hole range) > matches > sides > players, with games (CTP, skins, birdie payouts) stored on the round. That is exactly the BFE `rounds` model. The Cup's extra layer (teams, draft, draw) is the only Cup-only part. A non-team event (a 2-day member-guest, an outing) is the same record without `sides`.
2. **Number "matches" by part, from config.** `cupMatchNumbers` is now a function of rounds and segments. Any event with parts should get its labels the same way, never from hard-coded names.
3. **One host/player page per event, mode by URL + PIN.** `?event=...&host=1` plus the host PIN in session storage reveals host-only tabs (Money close, No-show, Brief). Keep that pattern; it needed no new login. Event cards expire after the event, so host links must also be reachable without the card (the wrap-up needed a typed URL). Idea: a "Host" entry on BFE-Admin per event that links `?host=1`.
4. **An "event wrap-up" should be a feature, not a checklist.** This session's wrap-up was manual: close Day 1 then Day 2 money (order matters because Day 1's unclaimed CTP rolls into Day 2's skins), print the Money tab, pay out, add a card to the app's Results list, write the story, save a frozen copy of the record, turn off the memories switch. A host "Finish event" action could do: reconcile (collect / paid / left over), freeze a snapshot, publish a results card from config, and retire the event banners.
5. **The Results list should be generated, not hand-edited.** The 11/11 Cup card was hand-added to `portal.html`. BFE events that finish should write a results-list entry (title, final line, link) that the app reads, like the Series cards do.
6. **A frozen snapshot per finished event.** The results page reads the live Worker record. A static `final.json` committed at wrap-up makes the historic page survive later edits. The results page already has "Download the results"; the missing half is "prefer the frozen file when it exists". (Open: Brian to send the download; see Dev-99.)
7. **Auto-story with a hand override works.** `bf-cup-results.html` writes a commentary from standings, and `bf-cup-story.json` replaces it when present. Keep both: the auto-story is right during the event, the hand story is the keepsake.
8. **Audit as a host tab.** Everything I did by hand this session (strokes per player, hole winners per nine, points, CTP/skins reconciliation, "collected = paid + left over") is engine output. A host-only Audit tab that prints it, with flags (gross 1 on a par 4, Outs, missing holes, Greensomes drive counts), removes the need for anyone to read raw JSON and gives Brian the "is it right?" answer in the app.
9. **Contingency features stay in the product, hidden.** The no-show model (`match.noshow`, walkover, mulligans) generalizes to any short-handed team event.
10. **Mobile realities:** names wrap to three lines in the team-score row on a phone; dialogs beat inserted cards; hide anything that needs reading; hard-refresh after each release.
