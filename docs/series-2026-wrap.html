Ladies and gentlemen, your champions

Nineteen players qualified for the Season Pot at $20 a head: $380 on the line, with the Overall crown and three flights up for grabs.
🏆 Overall Champion · $77
BJ Kolonia

And BJ Kolonia takes the whole thing! He sat third on the podium back in Series #3, climbed to second in #9, then came into the finale and won it outright at +3.65. A series score of +10.27 is the best in the field by nearly three and a half points. Add the Combo flight title and BJ walks off with $117 from the Season Pot alone. Just a masterclass in finishing.

Overall runners-up. Rich Potts finishes second at +6.78 for $46, and Jeff Rapp takes third at +6.56 for $30. Rapp did it the hard way: he teed it up in all seven events and finished above quota in four of them. That’s what we call showing up.

Green flight. Evan Lindermuth takes it at +4.71, and he saved his loudest work for Series #9, where he posted +9.28 over quota, the best single round of the entire season. Jordan Knappenberger and Bill Steirer complete the Green podium.

Gold flight. The new Gold tees brought only two qualifiers this year, so the unclaimed third-place money was spread across every other prize. Rich Penberg takes the title, and, folks, he played all seven events. Scott Skeens is right behind for second.

The season, event by event

Seven events, and here’s the one for the trivia night: seven different winners. Nobody won twice. This league is wide open.

Series #2 · May 3 · 17 players

Chooch Wernett
+2.44 · 2nd Mohamed Walli (+2.33) · 3rd Jeremy Burkett (+1.16)

Chooch opened the season with a photo finish. A tenth of a point separated him from Mohamed Walli, and he added a skin and a closest-to-the-pin on the 15th for a $105 day.

Series #3 · May 31 · 22 players

Adam Costa
+6.34 · 2nd Carl Stadtmueller (+4.41) · 3rd BJ Kolonia (+3.10)

The biggest field of the year, and Adam Costa ran away from it by nearly two points. Harry Pry grabbed a pair of skins to make some noise of his own.

Series #4 · June 14 · 20 players

Wilbur Hlay
+4.85 · 2nd Tom Stitt (+4.15) · 3rd Bill Steirer (+3.35)

Wilbur Hlay wins it and cashes a skin on top, for a $110 day, the biggest single-event payday of the season.

Series #5 · July 19 · 19 players

Rich Potts
+8.00 · 2nd Adam Costa (+5.88) · 3rd Paul Kametz (+5.64)

Rich Potts goes eight over quota and picks up a skin and a closest-to-the-pin on the 8th. Scott Justus quietly collected two skins in the same round.

Series #6 · August 16 · 20 players

Dave Sherwin
+8.39 · 2nd Bill Steirer (+6.21) · 3rd Jake Knappenberger (+5.46)

Dave Sherwin lights it up at +8.39, the second-best round of the season, and takes the closest-to-the-pin on 8 as a bonus.

Series #9 · October 25 · 20 players

Evan Lindermuth
+9.28 · 2nd BJ Kolonia (+6.58) · 3rd Tom Arnold (+4.90)

The round of the year. Evan’s +9.28 is the highest of the season, and he cashed a skin on top of it. Meanwhile Jordan Knappenberger picked up two skins and a $108 day.

Series #10 · November 1 · 13 players · The Finale

BJ Kolonia
+3.65 · 2nd Jordan Knappenberger (+2.77) · 3rd Tom Stitt (+1.69)

Thirteen players and one last shot at the title. BJ Kolonia closes the season with the win and locks up the Overall title. Jordan Knappenberger took three of the six skins for a $93 day, and Evan Lindermuth won both the 8th and the 18th closest-to-the-pin.
