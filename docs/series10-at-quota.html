#PlayerQuotaSeries
How to read this
  • Each registered player scores their Sunday quota exactly. Anyone who is not registered keeps the score they have.
  • "Series" is the series score after Sunday: best 4 results plus the average of the rest, each result being points minus quota.
  • "Was" under each name is the standing after Series #9, with the move up or down.
  • A round at quota helps players with five or six events more than players with four, because it replaces their weakest counted round. That is how the series formula works, and it is why a leader can slip with a quota day.
  • A flight is set by the average of the handicap index recorded at every event a player has played, Sunday's included: under 10.0 is Green, 10.0 to 17.9 is Combo, 18.0 and up is Gold. The tee played does not matter, so a player who has moved between tees keeps the flight their average sets. The average is shown under each name.
  • Quotas are from the preliminary groupings. If a handicap index changes before Sunday, a quota and its row change with it. Flights are as they stand now; the series re-sorts them after each event.
  • This is a projection from results through Series #9. Sunday's scores decide the standings.
