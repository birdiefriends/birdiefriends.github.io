v4.6.0 · 2026-09-24
Deployed: 2026-09-24 16:54 (Gatherings Close & Calculate: (1) The host of a gamed Gathering now gets a "🏁 Host · Close & Calculate" section at the bottom of the Live Panel. It checks who's turned in a scorecard (flags confirmed players with no card, and cards with blank holes), previews the payout, and closes with whoever actually played — pot = $/player × scorecards in. (2) Payout rules: Skins on GROSS strokes, outright low score only (tie = no skin, no carryover), $/skin = skin pot ÷ skins won, no skins won → pot given back evenly; CTP pays $/hole to each configured hole's leader, unclaimed holes roll into Skins; BirdieBall splits among everyone who kept it, or if nobody did, whoever held it longest (latest hole, then stroke) wins, ties split. Every split rounds DOWN to whole dollars; the remainder is shown, not redistributed. (3) Closed results appear as a "🏆 Game Results" block in that Gathering's My History story for every player; the host also gets "↩️ Reopen to fix & re-close" there, which brings the Live Panel back.)
